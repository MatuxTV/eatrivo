# Complaints Policy

Last updated: March 18, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This complaints policy explains how complaints related to digital services provided through the Eatrivo platform are handled.

## 2. When you can file a complaint

You may file a complaint if, for example:

- the service was not made available after payment,
- the service repeatedly fails due to a technical error,
- the delivered functionality does not match the ordered plan or service description,
- you were charged incorrectly or more than once.

## 3. How to file a complaint

Please send your complaint without undue delay to `support@eatrivo.sk`.

Include:

- the name and email of your account,
- the order number or payment reference, if available,
- a description of the issue,
- the date when the issue occurred,
- screenshots or any other relevant evidence.

## 4. Resolution timeframe

We will acknowledge your complaint without undue delay. The complaint will be resolved no later than 30 days from the date it was submitted, unless applicable law requires a shorter period.

## 5. How complaints are resolved

Depending on the case, we may resolve a complaint by:

- fixing the technical issue,
- providing replacement service,
- granting a reasonable discount,
- refunding the paid amount where remediation is not possible or proportionate.

## 6. Consumer rights

This complaints policy applies together with the terms of service and consumer rights under applicable Slovak and EU law.

## 7. Alternative dispute resolution

If you are not satisfied with how your complaint was handled, you may use the EU online dispute resolution platform: <https://ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr>.
